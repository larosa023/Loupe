## Loupe 0.1.0

The first release of Loupe, a fast, modern replacement for the Windows 11 Photos app.

### Viewing
- **Opens in a blink.** The first image starts decoding before the window exists, and JPEGs decode straight to screen size.
- **Flip without blank frames.** Arrow keys, Page Up/Down or the mouse wheel move through the folder, with the next and previous images already decoded. Sort by name, date taken, date modified or size.
- **Zoom** to fit or 100%, scroll-to-zoom at the cursor, drag to pan, double-click to toggle. Full resolution loads in the background when you zoom past the screen-sized image.
- **Animated GIF, WebP and APNG**, with pause and frame stepping.
- **Filmstrip** (F), **grid** of the whole folder (G), **info panel** (I) with camera, lens, exposure and GPS, and **compare** (C) with synced zoom and pan.
- **Slideshow** (Space) and full screen (F11).
- JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, BMP, TIFF, ICO, SVG, and RAW previews for CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW and DNG. Color profiles and EXIF orientation are applied.

### Editing
- Crop with preset ratios, rotate, flip, straighten, and exposure, contrast, saturation and warmth.
- Save asks before replacing the original, and Ctrl+Z can put the original back. Save a copy, or Revert.

### Files
- Copy image or path, show in Explorer, open in Strata, set as wallpaper, print, Open with.
- Delete to the Recycle Bin with undo, rename (F2), drag and drop.

### The suite
- The 14 suite themes, Match Hangar, accent colors and Hangar theme files, switchable live.
- Built-in updater with Stable and Beta channels, SHA-256 checks and automatic rollback.
- Registers as a default-app choice for every format it opens, without admin rights. Portable mode.
